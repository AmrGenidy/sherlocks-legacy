# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of a welcoming, harassment-free community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in *Sherlock's Legacy* a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming — especially to newcomers and first-time contributors.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and the players.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and whenever an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.
