# Contributors

*Sherlock's Legacy* is created and maintained by **Amr Mohamed**, who built and wrote all of it - the engine, the GUI and terminal, single-player and LAN multiplayer, the Case Maker, every bundled case, the translations, and the art direction.

Contributions from the community are welcome and warmly credited. When you contribute - code, a case, a translation, art, or a bug fix - add yourself under the matching section in your pull request. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.
